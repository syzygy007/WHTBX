Every signature placed through WHTBX carries an ID. Type it in and we show the signing record: the document name, who signed, when, and the fingerprint of the file. The document itself is never stored here.

On record.

To confirm a file is the executed copy, compute its SHA-256 (any hash tool) and compare it to the executed fingerprint above. A match means the file is byte for byte what was signed.
